Why is butter?
Butter is the golden treasure born from cream, carrying within it the warmth of sunlight, the richness of the earth, and the quiet magic of transformation. It melts like liquid gold, turning ordinary food into something worthy of celebration and filling every bite with a deep, velvety richness. Perhaps butter exists simply because the world, in all its complexity, needed one perfect little piece of edible luxury.
