Where is Butter?
Butter is found where the quiet richness of nature gathers, born from fresh cream and shaped by the simple rhythm of grazing fields, green grass, and morning light. It rests like a small golden stone of the countryside, carrying the earthy warmth of the land into every meal. In a way, butter is nature condensed into something soft, nourishing, and beautifully simple.
